## Corrections (applied in Notion 2026-10-05; renal column follows the UK SmPC, reviewer's choice)

### 1. Renal dose (table column): too low, about half the label dose ❌

| CrCl (mL/min) | Notion property | Daily total | UK SmPC (twice daily) | Consensus 2019 (Css,avg 2 mg/L) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 40–60 | 2 MIU q12h | 4 MIU | 30–50: **5.5–7.5 MIU** | 40–<50: 6.65 MIU; 50–<60: 7.40 MIU |
| 10–40 | 2 MIU q24h | 2 MIU | 10–30: **4.5–5.5 MIU** | 10–<20: 4.85; 20–<30: 5.30; 30–<40: 5.90 MIU |
| <10 | 1.5 MIU q36h | 1 MIU | **3.5 MIU** | 0: 3.95; 5–<10: 4.40 MIU |

The "1.5 … q36h" looks taken from the US label's "1.5 **mg/kg** q36h" (CrCl 10–29). That is mg CBA per kg, not MIU. For a 60 kg patient it is ≈ 90 mg CBA ≈ 2.7 MIU, and it applies to CrCl 10–29, not <10.

### 4. Electrolyte toxicity (pseudo-Bartter) missing ❌
US label (Warnings) and UK SmPC 4.4: renal tubulopathy with **hypokalemia, metabolic alkalosis, hypocalcemia, hypomagnesemia**; consider electrolyte monitoring. Not mentioned anywhere on the page. Add the `hypokalemia` side-effect tag and `electrolyte` to Monitor.

### 5. Inhaled high-dose "6 MIU q8h" ⚠️
That is 18 MIU/day. The UK inhalation maximum is **6 MIU/day** (1–2 MIU 2–3×/day). The page body gives 4.5 MIU q8h for VAP; the VAP trial cited in the consensus used **4 MIU q8h**. The column and the body disagree, and neither cites a source for 6 MIU q8h.

### 6. Pediatric dose (table column) incomplete ❌
- Inhaled "0.5–1 MIU q12h" is the UK dose for **<2 years** only. For ≥2 years it is 1–2 MIU 2–3×/day (max 6 MIU/day).
- IV dose missing. UK: ≤40 kg 75,000–150,000 IU/kg/day in 3 doses (>40 kg: adult dose). US: 2.5–5 mg CBA/kg/day in 2–4 doses.

### 7. CRRT missing from the table column ❌
The page body has the consensus dose (13.3 MIU/day = 6.65 MIU q12h). The UK SmPC says "as in normal renal function, three times daily". The column has no CRRT line.

## Verified ✅

- **IV adult dose:** 9 MIU loading dose, then 9 MIU/day in 2–3 doses (SmPC 4.2). The loading dose applies regardless of renal function, including RRT (SmPC).
- **Inhaled standard dose:** 1–2 MIU q8–12h, max 6 MIU/day (SmPC).
- **Mechanism:** binds LPS and disrupts the outer membrane; CMS is an inactive prodrug (US label, SmPC).
- **Coverage tags:** Pseudomonas, E. coli, Klebsiella, Enterobacter (US label). CRAB, CRKP and CRE are consistent with consensus use. Proteus and Neisseria are not covered (US label) ✅.
- **Side-effect tags:** AKI and neurotoxicity (US label, SmPC).
- **Unit conversions in the body:** 1 MIU ≈ 33 mg CBA ≈ 80 mg CMS (SmPC: 34 mg / 80 mg).
- **Intraventricular dose** 125,000 IU/day (SmPC 4.2).
- **"Polymyxin B preferred for routine systemic use; colistin preferred for lower UTI"** matches consensus R6–R7.
