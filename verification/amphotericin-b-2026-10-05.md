### A1 · Adult dose

<span color="green">`IV`</span> only — 50 mg/vial。⚠️ 確認產品名稱與劑量：Fungizone (conventional deoxycholate) ≠ AmBisome/Ampholipad (liposomal 3–5 mg/kg)，不可互換<br>Test dose: 1 mg in 20 mL D5W IV over 20–30 min；每 30 min 記錄 T/P/R/BP ×2–4 h (US/TW)；UK: observe ≥30 min<br>起始: 0.25 mg/kg/day；severe, rapidly progressive: 0.3 mg/kg/day；cardio-renal impairment 或 test dose 嚴重反應: 5–10 mg/day<br>Titration: 每日 ↑5–10 mg → usual 0.5–0.7 mg/kg/day (US/TW)；UK: 0.5–1.0 mg/kg/day，seriously ill 可漸增至 1.5 mg/kg/day (高劑量可 QOD)<br>Max: 1.0 mg/kg/day 或 1.5 mg/kg QOD；任何情況每日總量絕不可 >1.5 mg/kg (overdose → 致命性心跳/心肺停止)<br>停藥 >7 天 → 從 0.25 mg/kg 重新開始並逐步 titrate<br>IDSA: invasive candidiasis 0.5–0.7 mg/kg/day (C. glabrata/C. krusei 可至 1 mg/kg/day)；cryptococcal meningitis induction 0.7–1.0 mg/kg/day + flucytosine 100 mg/kg/day ≥2 wk (HIV；non-HIV non-transplant ≥4 wk)；fluconazole-resistant Candida cystitis/pyelonephritis (C. glabrata, C. krusei) 0.3–0.6 mg/kg/day ×1–7 d<br>Total dose/duration (label): sporotrichosis ≤9 mo (total ≤2.5 g)；aspergillosis ≤11 mo (total ≤3.6 g)；rhinocerebral mucormycosis cumulative ≥3 g<br>Febrile neutropenia empirical (TW 仿單 only): 1 mg/kg/day (仿單原文「推薦靜脈輸注濃度為 1 mg/kg/day (1 mg/10 mL)」混用劑量與濃度，疑為翻譯錯誤)<br>給藥: SWFI 10 mL 泡製 (5 mg/mL) → D5W (pH >4.2) 稀釋至 0.1 mg/mL，infuse over 2–6 h；不可用 NS 或含 bacteriostatic agent 之稀釋液 (沉澱)；in-line filter 孔徑 ≥1.0 µm；避免 <1 h 快速輸注

**Why:** Every dosing element is taken from the labels. The US boxed warning and D&A cover the 1.5 mg/kg cap, test dose, 0.25/0.3 mg/kg start, 5–10 mg start in cardio-renal impairment, titration to 0.5–0.7, maximum 1.0/day or 1.5 QOD, 2–6 h infusion at 0.1 mg/mL, 'Do not reconstitute with saline solutions', the >1.0 micron filter and the total-dose figures. The TW insert section 3 matches the US text word for word in substance, and I read the febrile-neutropenia sentence on the PDF myself. UK SmPC 4.2 covers the non-equivalence of amphotericin products, titration to 1.0, and up to 1.5 mg/kg in seriously ill patients. US Precautions cover the >7-day interruption rule. Disease-specific doses come from IDSA candidiasis 2016 (typical 0.5–0.7, up to 1 mg/kg for C. glabrata/krusei; 0.3–0.6 mg/kg ×1–7 d for fluconazole-resistant cystitis/pyelonephritis) and IDSA cryptococcal 2010 (0.7–1.0 mg/kg + flucytosine 100 mg/kg/day ≥2 wk in HIV, ≥4 wk in non-HIV non-transplant). ### A2 · Renal dose, HD, CRRT

仿單無 CrCl 劑量調整表 (US/UK/TW)；TW 仿單: 血中濃度不受腎/肝疾病影響；但 nephrotoxicity 為主要劑量限制毒性<br>Cardio-renal impairment: 起始 5–10 mg/day，依腎功能每日 ↑5–10 mg (US/TW)；慎用並頻繁監測 SCr<br>UK SmPC: SCr >260 µmol/L (≈2.9 mg/dL) → 停藥或顯著減量直到腎功能改善<br>腎功能不全或高風險 (併用腎毒性藥、利尿劑) → 考慮改用 lipid AmB (IDSA 2010)<br>避免 <1 h 快速輸注 (hyperkalemia、arrhythmia，腎功能不全者尤甚) (UK/TW)<br>HD: 不可透析 (>90% protein bound, poorly dialyzable) → 不需補充劑量 (US/UK/TW)<br>CRRT: 仿單無資料；高蛋白結合、透析清除差 → 不需調整，usual dose (Trotman 2005 PMID 16163635: 0.4–1 mg/kg q24h ⚠️ 表格數值未能核對全文)

**Why:** No label has a CrCl table. The US label says: 'should be used with care in patients with reduced renal function; frequent monitoring of renal function', start 5–10 mg in impaired cardio-renal function, and 'not hemodialyzable', '>90% bound… poorly dialyzable'. UK SmPC 4.4 says: 'If serum creatinine exceeds 260 micromol/L the drug should be discontinued or the dosage markedly reduced until renal function is improved', and that rapid infusion under 1 h in renal insufficiency causes hyperkalaemia/arrhythmias. TW insert 9.2.3 says: 「血液濃度並不會受到腎臟或肝臟疾病的影響」. The source brief and the TW transcription .txt both missed that sentence. Section 8 says it cannot be removed by haemodialysis. IDSA 2010 recommends lipid AmB in patients with or predisposed to renal dysfunction. For CRRT, no label covers it. I checked the Trotman 2005 PMID with esummary, but full text was not reachable, so the orchestrator should confirm the 0.4–1 mg/kg q24h table value before writing it. If it cannot be confirmed, keep only 'no adjustment (label: poorly dialyzable, >90% protein bound)'. The US label is used per the hierarchy (TW insert = US text), with the UK value given alongside.

### A9 · Mechanism

Polyene: 與真菌細胞膜 sterol (ergosterol) 結合 → 膜通透性改變、細胞內成分滲漏 → fungistatic 或 fungicidal (依體液濃度及菌種感受性) / binds sterols in fungal cell membrane → leakage of intracellular components<br>人體細胞膜亦含 sterol → 人體與真菌細胞損傷可能有共同機轉 (毒性來源)<br>UK SmPC: 於可達之體液濃度下 fungistatic rather than fungicidal

**Why:** US Pharmacokinetics/TW 9.1: 'fungistatic or fungicidal depending on the concentration… binding to sterols in the cell membrane… leakage of intracellular components. Mammalian cell membranes also contain sterols'. UK 5.1 says 'fungistatic rather than fungicidal in concentrations obtainable in body fluids'. I did not use the hospital site's 'oxidation-dependent macrophage stimulation' because no label states it.

### A11 · Pregnancy

No FDA letter category (FDA 已取消字母分級)。動物生殖研究未見胎兒傷害；少數孕婦全身性黴菌感染以 AmB 成功治療、無明顯胎兒影響，但案例數少 (US/TW)<br>US: 僅於明確需要時使用；UK/TW: 效益大於潛在風險時才使用<br>IDSA 2010: 孕婦播散性/CNS cryptococcosis 使用 AmBd 或 lipid AmB ± flucytosine；fluconazole 避免於第一孕期
