### A9 · Mechanism

Binds PBPs (transpeptidases) → inhibits cell-wall synthesis → bactericidal (US FDA; SmPC 5.1). Active against some penicillinases/cephalosporinases, but hydrolysed by ESBL, derepressed AmpC and carbapenemases. Active metabolite desacetyl-cefotaxime. Time-dependent killing (target %fT>MIC; Wieringa 2022 PMID 36210582).

**Why:** The column is empty. US FDA Microbiology, Mechanism of Action: 'bactericidal agent that acts by inhibition of bacterial cell wall synthesis… activity in the presence of some beta-lactamases'. The same section says most ESBL- and carbapenemase-producing isolates are resistant. SmPC 5.1 covers PBP inhibition and resistance through ESBL/AmpC, PBP alteration, permeability and efflux. FDA Clinical Pharmacology says the desacetyl metabolite contributes bactericidal activity.

**Sources:** US FDA label – CLINICAL PHARMACOLOGY, Microbiology – https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=434fb630-c8d6-4583-b3ee-8e4ba6552c3d; UK SmPC 5.1 – https://www.medicines.org.uk/emc/product/100184/smpc; Wieringa A et al. JAC 2022, PMID 36210582 – https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36210582/

### A13 · Notes

可活能 Claforan 1 g/vial (衛署藥輸字第022319號). Na 48 mg (2.09 mmol)/g (仿單; SmPC) [US label: 50.5 mg = 2.2 mEq/g]. t½ ~1 h (desacetyl metabolite ~1.3 h); 2.5–3.6 h when CrCl 3–10. Protein binding 25–40%. Good CSF penetration with inflamed meninges (3–30 µg/mL) (SmPC 5.2). Rapid IV bolus (<60 s) via central line → life-threatening arrhythmia: push over 3–5 min (US FDA; SmPC). NO coverage: P. aeruginosa, Enterococcus, MRSA, Listeria, Acinetobacter, Stenotrophomonas, atypicals (SmPC 5.1 inherently resistant) [outdated claims: US FDA label still lists Pseudomonas (indications) and Acinetobacter/Enterococcus (in-vitro list; footnote says Enterococcus may be intrinsically resistant); 仿單 claims in-vitro Pseudomonas activity and >6 g/day for Pseudomonas]. ESBL producers always resistant; avoid for AmpC-inducible Enterobacter/C. freundii even if susceptible in vitro (SmPC 5.1; IDSA 2024 PMID 39108079). Complete cross-resistance with ceftriaxone (SmPC). Anaerobes unreliable → add metronidazole for IAI (SmPC 4.2). No Chlamydia activity → add anti-chlamydial for PID (US FDA). Other label indications without tags: gonorrhoea (exclude syphilis, SmPC; CDC 2021 prefers ceftriaxone); borreliosis/Lyme (SmPC; Jarisch-Herxheimer possible). Endocarditis (SmPC): only for penicillin-allergic (non-type 1) patients, in combination. IM: lidocaine solution never IV.

**Why:** The column is empty. This gathers clinically important label content that has no column of its own. Sodium: Taiwan insert 組成 and SmPC 4.4 give 48 mg (2.09 mmol)/g; US FDA DESCRIPTION gives 50.5 mg (2.2 mEq)/g. PK from SmPC 5.2. Arrhythmia warning from US FDA WARNINGS and SmPC 4.2/4.4. Resistance from SmPC 5.1 ('INHERENTLY RESISTANT': Enterococcus, Listeria, MRSA, Acinetobacter, P. aeruginosa, Stenotrophomonas; ESBL 'always resistant'; AmpC-inducible pathogens 'should not be treated with cefotaxime'). ### B9 · Mechanism

Binds PBPs (transpeptidases) → inhibits cell wall synthesis → bactericidal (FDA; SmPC 5.1). Active against some penicillinases/cephalosporinases, but hydrolysed by ESBL, AmpC (derepressed/inducible) and carbapenemases (FDA microbiology; SmPC 5.1). Resistance also comes from PBP alteration (pneumococci; MRSA extra low-affinity PBP), reduced permeability and efflux (SmPC 5.1). Complete cross-resistance with ceftriaxone (SmPC 5.1). Active metabolite: desacetyl-cefotaxime (15–25%; SmPC 5.2). Time-dependent killing; ICU target fT>MIC 100% (Wieringa 2022, PMID 36210582). Routine β-lactam TDM in critically ill patients recommended (ESICM/ESCMID position paper, PMID 32383061).
