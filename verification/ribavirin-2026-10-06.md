### A7 · Side Effects

anemia, hematologic, neutropenia, thrombocytopenia, hyperuricemia, CNS, GI, arrhythmia, SJS/TEN, hypersensitivity, lactic acidosis

**Why:** Existing options only. anemia/hematologic: haemolytic anaemia is the main toxicity (boxed warning). Hb \<10 g/dL in up to 14% (TW), onset in weeks 1–2. Neutropenia and thrombocytopenia mostly come from the interferon combination (TW Table 2, §8.1 grade 3–4 neutropenia 28%). hyperuricemia: haemolysis raises uric acid and indirect bilirubin, with gout risk (UK 4.4/4.8, TW §8.1). CNS: depression, insomnia, headache, suicidal ideation (TW Table 4, US 5.10; largely interferon-driven). GI: nausea, anorexia, vomiting (also with DAA+RBV, UK 4.8). arrhythmia: supraventricular arrhythmia, and anaemia can precipitate MI (TW §5.1, UK 4.4, US 5.2). SJS/TEN and hypersensitivity: SJS/TEN/EM reported (US §4, TW §8.1); acute hypersensitivity (UK 4.4). lactic acidosis: with didanosine/NRTIs (US §4, §7.1; TW §7). Interferon-only effects (injection-site reaction, flu-like symptoms) are left out; mention them in the body as combination-regimen effects. Pancreatitis, pulmonary and dental disorders have no option and go in Notes/body.

**Sources:** DailyMed ribavirin Boxed Warning; §5.2; §6.1 'The primary toxicity of ribavirin is hemolytic anemia'; §4 SJS/TEN; §7.1 https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=35f99f76-f2ef-4a81-91ff-285419664be3; UK SmPC §4.4 'Uric acid may increase with ribavirin due to haemolysis'; §4.8 'with DAA… anaemia, nausea, vomiting, asthenia, fatigue, insomnia, cough, dyspnoea, pruritus and rash' https://www.medicines.org.uk/emc/product/7108/smpc; Taiwan insert §8.1 表四; '達14%的病人血紅素濃度降至低於10 g/dl'; '多形紅斑、史帝文強生徵候群…毒性上皮壞死溶解'; '尿酸及間歇性膽紅素升高' https://mcp.fda.gov.tw/im_detail_1/%E8%A1%9B%E7%BD%B2%E8%97%A5%E8%A3%BD%E5%AD%97%E7%AC%AC048027%E8%99%9F

### A10 · Drug Interactions

### B9 · Mechanism

Synthetic purine (guanosine) nucleoside analogue. The anti-HCV mechanism is not fully understood (FDA 12.4; TW仿單 10.1). Ribavirin triphosphate inhibits HCV polymerase in biochemical assays and raises the mutation rate of RNA virus genomes (FDA 12.4). It inhibits host IMPDH (FDA 7.4). Monotherapy has no effect on HCV-RNA or histology (SmPC 5.1). PK: F ≈ 64%; high-fat meal ↑ AUC/Cmax 70%; not protein bound; Vd about 2825 L (taken into RBCs and other cells via nucleoside transporter); about 6× accumulation; steady state at about 4 wk; multiple-dose t½ ≈ 298 h (about 12 d); renal excretion (FDA 12.3)

**Why:** US 11: 'synthetic nucleoside analogue (purine analogue)'. US 12.4: 'mechanism… not fully understood… increases the mutation frequency… ribavirin triphosphate inhibits HCV polymerase in a biochemical reaction'. US 7.4: 'Ribavirin is known to inhibit IMDH'. US 12.3 PK values: F 64%, half-life 298 h, Vd 2825 L, food +70%, 6-fold accumulation, no protein binding. The boxed warning gives a 'multiple-dose half-life of 12 days'. SmPC 5.1: monotherapy had no effect.
