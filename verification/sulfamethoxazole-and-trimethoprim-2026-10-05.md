### A9 · Mechanism

Sequential folate-pathway blockade. SMX (sulfonamide) competes with PABA → inhibits dihydropteroate synthase (dihydrofolate synthesis). TMP reversibly inhibits bacterial dihydrofolate reductase (DHF → THF). Blocks two consecutive steps of purine/nucleic-acid synthesis; synergistic, bacteriostatic → may be bactericidal depending on conditions. TMP affinity for bacterial DHFR is ~50,000× that for the mammalian enzyme.

**Why:** Empty column; the content is taken directly from the label microbiology/pharmacodynamics sections.

**Sources:** US FDA injection label §12.4 'Sulfamethoxazole inhibits bacterial synthesis of dihydrofolic acid by competing with para-aminobenzoic acid (PABA). Trimethoprim… reversibly inhibiting… dihydrofolate reductase… blocks two consecutive steps', https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=ebb570bb-6e5e-489e-875c-9e7b3a6d716a; UK SmPC infusion §5.1 'resulting in bacteriostasis… Depending on the conditions the effect may be bactericidal… affinity for mammalian DHFR is some 50,000 times less', https://www.medicines.org.uk/emc/product/4669/smpc

### B9 · Mechanism

SMX: competes with PABA → inhibits dihydropteroate synthase (↓ dihydrofolate). TMP: reversibly inhibits bacterial DHFR (↓ tetrahydrofolate). Sequential blockade of folate → nucleic acid synthesis; synergistic; may be bactericidal (UK).

**Why:** The column is empty. UK SmPC 5.1: 'Sulfamethoxazole competitively inhibits the utilisation of para-aminobenzoic acid… resulting in bacteriostasis. Sulfacotrim TW insert §10.1 adds '同時個別阻斷病菌在合成葉酸時所需之兩種酵素'.
