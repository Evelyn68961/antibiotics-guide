## Product and sources

Two products stocked at FJUH and given together as one long-acting regimen: VOC02 Vocabria (cabotegravir) 600 mg/3 mL prolonged-release IM suspension, 莫帕滋長效注射劑, 衛部藥輸字第028218號, NHI BC28218216, ATC J05AJ04; and REK01 Rekambys (rilpivirine) 900 mg/3 mL (300 mg/mL) prolonged-release IM suspension, 瑞卡必, 衛部藥輸字第028217號, NHI BC28217216, ATC J05AG05. The US equivalent is the CABENUVA co-pack (DailyMed setid 1698baf3-f895-4c42-a1b1-e9ee3f20da36, v16, SPL effective 2025-11-26, published Dec 05 2025). UK SmPCs: Vocabria 600 mg inj (eMC 12957, rev 15 Apr 2026) and Rekambys 900 mg (eMC 12950, rev 13/11/2025). Only the 3-mL vials (600 mg and 900 mg) appear among the hospital codes. The Notion page was blank: only the title and Category were filled in.

### A1 · Adult dose

<span color="orange">`IM`</span> gluteal only (ventrogluteal preferred; dorsogluteal acceptable). Cabotegravir (CAB) + rilpivirine (RPV) are always given together at the same visit, at separate gluteal sites (opposite sides or ≥2 cm apart). Complete regimen for virologically suppressed adults (HIV-1 RNA <50 copies/mL)<br>• Optional oral lead-in <span color="blue">`PO`</span>: CAB 30 mg + RPV 25 mg qd with a meal for ~1 month (≥28 d), or go straight to injection. First injection on the last day of the oral lead-in or of current ART<br>• Every 2 months (q2m): CAB 600 mg (3 mL) + RPV 900 mg (3 mL) at month 1 and month 2, then every 2 months from month 4<br>• Monthly: CAB 600 mg (3 mL) + RPV 900 mg (3 mL) once; from 1 month later, CAB 400 mg (2 mL) + RPV 600 mg (2 mL) monthly<br>• Dosing window ±7 d. Monthly→q2m: give 600/900 mg 1 month after the last monthly dose, then every 2 months. q2m→monthly: give 400/600 mg 2 months after the last q2m dose, then monthly<br>• Planned missed visit (>7 d): oral CAB 30 mg + RPV 25 mg qd (or any fully suppressive oral ART) can replace up to 2 monthly visits or 1 q2m visit. **Why:** New entry with an empty column. The dosing schedules are identical in the US label, UK SmPC and Taiwan insert. ### A5 · Indications

HIV

**Why:** Approved use: treatment of HIV-1 in adults and adolescents (≥12 y, ≥35 kg), as a complete regimen replacing current ART in people who are virologically suppressed (<50 copies/mL) on a stable regimen, with no history of treatment failure and no known or suspected resistance to CAB or RPV. The UK wording is no prior virological failure with, and no resistance to, NNRTIs or integrase inhibitors. Do NOT add 'HIV PrEP': Vocabria/Rekambys are not licensed for PrEP.

### A9 · Mechanism

Cabotegravir = INSTI: binds the integrase active site and blocks the strand-transfer step of HIV DNA integration. Rilpivirine = diarylpyrimidine NNRTI: non-competitive inhibition of HIV-1 reverse transcriptase (does not inhibit human DNA polymerases α/β/γ). Long-acting nanosuspensions with absorption-limited apparent t½: CAB 5.6–11.5 wk, RPV 13–28 wk; Tmax CAB ~7 d, RPV 3–4 d. Clearance: CAB mainly UGT1A1 (UGT1A9 minor); RPV mainly CYP3A. Protein binding >99%

### B7 · Side Effects

hypersensitivity, SJS/TEN, DRESS, LFT↑, CNS, GI, weight gain, IRIS

**Why:** All are existing schema options, each matched to label text. Hypersensitivity, SJS/TEN (CAB) and DRESS (RPV) are in US 5.1. LFT↑ is hepatotoxicity (US 5.3). CNS covers headache, dizziness, sleep disorders and depressive disorders/suicidality (US 5.4/6.1). GI is nausea, vomiting, abdominal pain and diarrhoea.
