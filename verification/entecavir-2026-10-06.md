### A9 · Mechanism

Deoxyguanosine nucleoside analogue → phosphorylated intracellularly to entecavir-triphosphate (intracellular t½ ~15 h), which competes with dGTP and inhibits all 3 activities of HBV polymerase/reverse transcriptase: (1) base priming, (2) reverse transcription of the negative strand from pregenomic RNA, (3) positive-strand HBV DNA synthesis. Weak inhibitor of cellular DNA polymerases α, β, δ and mitochondrial γ. Not a CYP450 substrate, inhibitor or inducer. Resistance needs '2 hits': lamivudine-resistance rtM204I/V ± rtL180M **plus** rtT184, rtS202 or rtM250. Cumulative 5-y genotypic resistance: ~1.2% in nucleoside-naïve vs ~51% in lamivudine-refractory patients. Activity against HIV is weak: selects M184I in cell culture, and M184V variants lose susceptibility

**Why:** The column is empty. Text follows US §12.4 'Mechanism of Action' (deoxyguanosine analogue; triphosphate t½ 15 h; inhibits base priming, reverse transcription of the negative strand, and positive-strand synthesis; Ki 18 to >160 µM for polymerases α/β/δ/γ), §12.4 Resistance (cumulative rtT184/S202/M250 probability 1.2% at wk 240 in naïve vs 51.5% in lamivudine-refractory) and §12.3 (not a CYP substrate). UK 4.4 gives 6/15/36/47/51% at years 1–5 in lamivudine-refractory patients.

### A13 · Notes

本院品項: BAR03 Baraclude 0.5 mg (貝樂克膜衣錠), ENV01 Envir 0.5 mg, ENV02 Envir 1 mg (欣苷膜衣錠) — tablets only, 無口服液 (renal doses <0.5 mg are not possible → use interval dosing)<br>⚠️ **Boxed warning (US/TW 警語):** (1) severe acute **HBV exacerbation after stopping** → monitor LFT clinically + by lab for at least several months (UK ≥6 mo; AASLD: every 3 mo for ≥1 y); may need to restart therapy. ### B9 · Mechanism

Carbocyclic **deoxyguanosine nucleoside analogue** (鳥嘌呤核苷類似物). Phosphorylated intracellularly to entecavir-triphosphate (intracellular t½ ~15 h), which competes with dGTP and blocks all 3 HBV polymerase (RT) activities: base priming, reverse transcription of the negative strand from pgRNA, and positive-strand HBV DNA synthesis. Weak inhibitor of cellular DNA pol α/β/δ and mitochondrial pol γ. PK: 62–73% excreted unchanged in urine (GF + tubular secretion); terminal t½ 128–149 h, effective accumulation t½ ~24 h; protein binding ~13%; not a CYP450 substrate, inhibitor or inducer. Resistance is 'two-hit': lamivudine-resistance substitutions (rtM204I/V ± rtL180M) plus rtT184/rtS202/rtM250 → ~1.2% at 5 y in naïve patients vs ~51% in lamivudine-refractory patients. Selects HIV M184I/V

**Why:** Mechanism, PK and resistance figures were re-verified against US 12.3/12.4 and TW 10.x/11. The UK SmPC gives the same 5-year cumulative resistance rates in lamivudine-refractory patients (6/15/36/47/51%).

**Sources:** US FDA label §12.3 Pharmacokinetics, §12.4 Microbiology — https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=d59e9ea4-09ea-47bd-af2f-39f7a2e2aceb; TW insert Baraclude 藥動學/微生物學 sections — https://mcp.fda.gov.tw/im_detail_1/%E8%A1%9B%E7%BD%B2%E8%97%A5%E8%BC%B8%E5%AD%97%E7%AC%AC024469%E8%99%9F; UK SmPC §4.4 Resistance — https://www.medicines.org.uk/emc/product/213/smpc
