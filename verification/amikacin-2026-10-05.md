## Agreed fixes applied in Notion (57)

### A1 · Pregnancy (error)

**Was:** Category D — potential fetal ototoxicity/nephrotoxicity; use only if no alternative

**Now:** Aminoglycosides can cause fetal harm (US label WARNINGS; UK SmPC 4.6). Amikacin crosses the placenta: fetal peak ≈16% of maternal peak (US label). Total irreversible bilateral congenital deafness reported with streptomycin. Rat/mouse studies: no fetal harm; no well-controlled human studies (US). Use only when clearly needed; tell the patient about the fetal hazard (UK SmPC 4.6). 僅於明確需要時使用；台灣仿單無懷孕資料。

**Why:** FDA letter categories are retired, so 'Category D' must not appear as a current category. The US label's current wording is 'can cause fetal harm when administered to a pregnant woman'. The Taiwan insert has no pregnancy section, so the US label and SmPC are the sources.

**Sources:** US FDA label (DailyMed, Sagent) – WARNINGS; Clinical Pharmacology 'General' (placental transfer 16%) – https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=f260ff2a-76a0-4672-9516-91c344b67890; UK SmPC 4.6 – https://www.medicines.org.uk/emc/product/3784/smpc

### A2 · Page body (error)

**Was:** ### Pregnancy: 'AU TGA pregnancy category: D' / 'It has been shown that use of amikacin in pregnant women caused some babies to be born with problems.'
