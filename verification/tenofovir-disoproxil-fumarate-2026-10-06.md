### A9 · Mechanism

**Nucleotide** RT inhibitor: TDF is an acyclic nucleoside phosphonate diester analogue of AMP. Diester hydrolysis gives tenofovir, which cellular kinases phosphorylate to tenofovir diphosphate. This competes with dATP, inhibits HIV-1 RT and HBV polymerase, and acts as an obligate DNA chain terminator. It is a weak inhibitor of mammalian DNA pol α, β and mitochondrial γ (FDA 12.4). Oral bioavailability ~25% fasted; t½ ~17 h; not a CYP substrate; renal elimination by glomerular filtration + active tubular secretion via hOAT1/hOAT3/MRP4; 70–80% recovered unchanged in urine after IV tenofovir (FDA 12.3; UK 4.4/5.2). Resistance: HIV K65R, K70E; no HBV substitution associated with TDF resistance up to 384 weeks (FDA 12.4)
