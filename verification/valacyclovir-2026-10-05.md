### A9 · Mechanism

L-valyl ester prodrug of acyclovir → rapidly converted (first-pass, valaciclovir hydrolase) to acyclovir; bioavailability ≈54%, 3.3–5.5× that of oral acyclovir → phosphorylated by viral TK (HSV/VZV), then by cellular kinases → acyclovir triphosphate competitively inhibits viral DNA polymerase, is incorporated into viral DNA and causes chain termination
