### A12 · Breastfeeding

Short-term use acceptable (LactMed): milk levels are low (estimated infant intake ~6% of the maternal weight-adjusted dose) and calcium in milk inhibits infant absorption. Avoid prolonged (>21 d) or repeat courses; monitor the infant for rash, diarrhoea, thrush/diaper rash. Alternative: tetracycline (LactMed). US FDA: short-term use not necessarily contraindicated; effects of prolonged exposure unknown. UK SmPC 4.3/4.6: contraindicated in nursing mothers

**Why:** New entry. LactMed Summary: "there is not likely to be harm in short-term use of doxycycline during lactation because milk levels are low and absorption by the infant is inhibited by the calcium in breastmilk... As a theoretical precaution, avoid prolonged (>21 days) or repeat courses during nursing. Monitor the infant for rash and... diarrhea or candidiasis (thrush, diaper rash)." Drug Levels: "about 6% of the maternal weight-adjusted dosage". Alternate Drugs: "Tetracycline". US Nursing Mothers: "Short-term use by lactating women is not necessarily contraindicated". UK 4.3: "contraindicated in nursing mothers". The brief said the LactMed summary was cut off, but the JSON holds the full summary, including the >21-day threshold, so this text is verified.

### B9 · Mechanism
